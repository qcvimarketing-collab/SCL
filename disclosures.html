Commercial Brokerage Disclosure

Superb Choice Lending LLC operates as a commercial finance brokerage. We facilitate transactions between borrowers and a network of third-party institutional and private capital sources. We may receive compensation from lending partners upon successful placement.

State Licensing

Where required, Superb Choice Lending holds or operates under appropriate state licenses for commercial finance brokerage activity. Specific state license information is available upon request.

No Guarantees

We do not guarantee that any specific loan or financing program will be approved, funded, or available at any specific terms. All financing is subject to lender underwriting, borrower qualification, and market conditions at the time of application.

Equal Credit Opportunity

Superb Choice Lending and the lenders we work with comply with the Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA) and applicable fair lending laws. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, national origin, sex, marital status, age, or because all or part of an applicant's income comes from any public assistance program.

Cost of Capital Disclosure

Costs of commercial financing vary widely by program, borrower profile, and use of funds. Working capital advances may carry equivalent APRs significantly higher than traditional bank loans. We disclose all applicable rates, fees, and total cost-of-capital figures in writing before any borrower signs a final agreement.

Fair Lending Statement

We are committed to providing fair, transparent, and ethical lending placement services. Concerns regarding fair lending practices may be directed to advisors@superbchoicelending.com.
