Privacy Policy

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when you submit applications, schedule consultations, or subscribe to our communications. This includes contact information, business details, financial information necessary for underwriting, and authentication data for secure submission.

How We Use Your Information

Information is used solely to evaluate your financing requests, communicate with you regarding your application, and meet legal and regulatory obligations. We do not sell your information to third parties.

Sharing With Lending Partners

To place capital, we share relevant application information with our network of vetted lending partners. Each partner is bound by confidentiality and applicable data protection laws. You authorize this sharing when you submit an application.

Data Security

We employ industry-standard encryption for data in transit and at rest. Sensitive identifiers (SSN, tax ID) are encrypted at submission and stored in secured systems with access limited to underwriting personnel.

Your Rights

You may request access to your information, request corrections, or request deletion at any time by contacting advisors@superbchoicelending.com. Certain information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate business records.

Cookies & Analytics

This website uses minimal essential cookies for functionality. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or behavioral tracking.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy can be directed to advisors@superbchoicelending.com or by mail to our Boca Raton office.

Last updated: 2026. This policy may be updated periodically. Material changes will be communicated via email or notice on our website.
